# Jio Platforms IPO: status as of 2026-10-28

Tier tags: T1 = company / SEBI / regulator document. T3 = press (locate only, attribute in copy).

## Status
Listed today. Jio Platforms Ltd (JPL) debuted on NSE and BSE on 28 Oct 2026 at Rs 1,358 per share, about 21.4% above the Rs 1,119 issue price (T3, price and listing are press-reported, not yet checked against an exchange notice or the Prospectus).

## Documents (T1)
- DRHP dated 19 Jun 2026, filed with SEBI: 100% book-built FRESH ISSUE of up to 27.00 crore (270,000,000) equity shares of Rs 10 face value; Offer for Sale "Not applicable"; promoter Reliance Industries; lead manager listed first is Kotak Mahindra Capital; includes an "Eligible RIL Shareholders" reservation. Price left blank. https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/commondocs/jun-2026/Jio%20Platforms%20Ltd_p.PDF (summary, 13 pp, saved in raw/). Full DRHP also hosted by Jio: https://myjiostatic.cdn.jio.com/cms/assets/iv3/Jio%20Platforms%20Limited%20-%20DRHP.pdf (not downloaded).
- DRHP restated consolidated financials: revenue from operations Rs 146,885 cr (FY26), 128,218 cr (FY25), 109,558 cr (FY24); EBITDA 76,255 / 64,170 / 54,959 cr; PAT 30,049 / 26,109 / 21,423 cr; net leverage 0.36x; customers 524.4 mn (Mar 2026); ARPU exit quarter Rs 214.0.
- RIL Q1 FY27 media release, 17 Jul 2026 (Mukesh Ambani): "During the quarter, Jio Platforms Limited filed its DRHP with SEBI, a significant step towards its public listing." https://rilstaticasset.akamaized.net/sites/default/files/2026-07/Media_Release_RIL_Q1_FY2026-27_Financial_and_Operational_Performance.pdf
- AGM 29 Aug 2025 (48th RIL AGM): "We are aiming to list Jio by the first half of 2026, subject to all necessary approvals" (T3 quote via Storyboard18 https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/reliance-jio-eyes-ipo-in-first-half-of-2026-chairman-mukesh-ambani-79861.htm and Business Today 29 Aug 2025). The H1 2026 target slipped: the DRHP came on 19 Jun 2026 and listing on 28 Oct 2026 (Business Today 17 Jun 2026, citing FT, called the H1 timeline missed).
